Our Home Galaxy

The Milky Way

The Milky Way is a barred spiral galaxy. Stars, gas and dust circle a bright central bar and a supermassive black hole called Sagittarius A*. Our Sun sits in a smaller arm, the Orion Spur, about halfway out from the center.

      Our Cosmic Timeline

      From the Big Bang to Today

      1. 13.8 billion years ago
        The Big Bang

        Space, time and matter begin to expand.

      2. About 13.6 billion years ago
        First stars

        The first stars and galaxies light up.

      3. 4.6 billion years ago
        The Sun is born

        A cloud of gas collapses. Earth and the other planets follow.

      4. Over 3.5 billion years ago
        First life

        Microbes appear in Earth's oceans.

      5. About 300,000 years ago
        Modern humans

        Homo sapiens begins to look up at the night sky.

      6. 1969
        Footsteps on the Moon

        Apollo 11 lands and two astronauts walk on another world.

      Not to scale. Squeeze the universe's 13.8 billion years into one calendar year and modern humans show up about 11 minutes before midnight on December 31.
